# Market Research — Batch 1 (Health & Medical Trackers)
**Date:** July 2026 · **Cluster:** 2 of the 50-product catalog

## Why Cluster 2 first
The catalog's launch playbook recommends Cluster 2 or 3. Cluster 2 wins the tiebreak:
tracker-grid formats are the strongest fit for our programmatic pipeline, the caregiver
economy is a named 2026 standout niche, and health logs are evergreen (no January/gift
seasonality risk for a first launch).

## What the top sellers do (and what they miss)

### Blood pressure logs (most mature niche — proven demand, weak product quality)
Top sellers ([Sapphire Mountain Press](https://www.amazon.com/Blood-Pressure-Log-Book-Pulse/dp/107582432X),
[HA & S0 Large Print](https://www.amazon.com/Blood-Pressure-Log-Book-Tracking/dp/B0C1JGPP83),
[Pistachio Publishing](https://www.amazon.com/Blood-Pressure-Log-Book-Complete/dp/B08XFJ77XH)) converge on:
- **Large print** is its own winning sub-niche (senior buyers + adult-child gift buyers)
- 2–3 readings/day with systolic/diastolic/pulse columns; **undated** weekly layouts
- The best performer differentiates with **charts & graphs** ("Complete… with Charts & Graphs", 68 weeks)
- Reviews praise: bold printing, ample writing space, simple layouts, portability

**Gaps we exploit:** almost none combine BP tracking + medication adherence + monthly
plotted trend chart + doctor-visit prep pages in one book. Ours does all four.

### Caregiver logs (fast-growing, emotionally invested buyers)
Top sellers ([ClearCare Press](https://us.amazon.com/Caregiver-Daily-Log-Book-Appointments/dp/B0H3K51N4K),
[Compassionate Care Press](https://www.amazon.com/Caregiver-Daily-Log-Medications-Handoffs/dp/B0GLFT7VFH))
converge on: daily vitals + meds + meals + personal care + **shift/handoff notes** (the
differentiator that took over the niche in 2025–26).

**Gap we exploit:** nobody tracks the *caregiver*. Our weekly caregiver self-care
check-in ("you can't pour from an empty cup") is a review-magnet feature and the
emotional hook for the listing. We also add structured appointment prep pages.

### Digital planner market (Etsy) — from [SellerToolsHQ](https://sellertoolshq.com/guides/how-to-sell-planners-on-etsy/), [Planify](https://blog.planifypro.com/sell-planners-on-etsy/), [Paperbell](https://paperbell.com/blog/how-to-create-a-digital-planner-to-sell/)
- Niche-specific planners convert at **$4.99–$12.99**; $0.99 signals junk — **$7.99
  converts better than $1.99**. Charm pricing works ($8.99, not $9.00).
- Buyers expect instant-download PDFs that print clean on US Letter; test on-device.
- Listings convert on: first photo showing interior pages (not just cover), page-count
  and feature bullets, "undated — start anytime."

## Positioning rules distilled into the products
1. **Undated everywhere** — evergreen inventory, no expiry returns.
2. **Emotional cover/title language** ("Because you can't pour from an empty cup") over
   format language — per catalog rule 4.
3. **90-day / 12-week structures** matching real medical review cycles (A1C, post-op
   follow-up) — a hook competitors' "120 blank pages" cannot claim.
4. **Every book ends at the doctor's office**: visit-prep and summary pages turn a log
   into a communication tool — the single most under-served feature in every niche
   researched.
5. One accent color + one font family per book; section footers so interiors read as
   designed, not assembled (KDP quality filters + review photos).
