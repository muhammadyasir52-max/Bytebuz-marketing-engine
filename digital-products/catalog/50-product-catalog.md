## Launch Playbook (Condensed)

**Phase 1 — Validate (Week 1):** For each idea, search the exact title phrase on Amazon. Target: top results with BSR 5,000–50,000, under 300 reviews, and visibly weak covers/interiors on page 1.

**Phase 2 — Build (Weeks 2–6):** Start with ONE cluster (recommend Cluster 2 or 3 — health/mental health matches your Careline positioning and has the strongest 2026 keyword data). Publish 5–7 titles in that cluster before moving on. Canva Pro for interiors and covers; 8.5×11 planners, 100–120 pages.

**Phase 3 — Metadata:** Title = main keyword. Subtitle = the three-benefit formula (e.g., "Weekly Meal Planner with Grocery List & Budget Tracker"). Use all 7 backend keyword slots with audience variants (for women / for seniors / for beginners / large print).

**Phase 4 — Multiply:** Every winner gets variants: large print edition, teen edition, men's/women's covers, 6-month vs 12-month, and a matching Etsy digital version at 40–60% of the paperback price.

**Rules that protect you:**
- Never use dated interiors unless tied to academic/fiscal year — undated outsells dated
- Minimum 24 pages, even page count; avoid duplicate-template interiors (KDP filters them)
- One format per book (lined OR guided OR tracker grids — don't mix randomly)
- Emotional cover language beats format language: lead with where the buyer is right now
